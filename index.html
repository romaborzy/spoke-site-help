Common questions

Recording doesn't start. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings › Spoke. The first run in a language downloads the speech model; keep the iPhone online until it finishes.

Some filler words aren't counted. On-device recognition sometimes removes sounds like “um”. Words such as “like” or your own words in Settings › Filler words are still counted.

Restore a subscription. Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription. Settings › Manage Subscription in the app, or iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Where are my runs? Only on your iPhone, and in its iCloud backup if backup is on. Deleting the app deletes them.
