Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Spoke records and recognizes speech on your iPhone. Audio, transcripts, and numbers stay on the device. There is no account and no server.

What Spoke collects

Nothing. Spoke has no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere. The developer never receives your recordings, transcripts, or numbers.

What stays on your iPhone

Each run keeps its topic, date, length, the recognized words with their timing, the pauses, and, if Keep audio is on, the audio. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, these are part of that backup under your Apple Account.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used only while you record a run. Recognition runs on the device with Apple's on-device speech model. The first time you use a language, iOS may download that model from Apple.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Spoke only checks with the App Store whether Speech Pro is active. Spoke never sees your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete one run by swiping it, or all runs in Settings › Delete All Runs. Turn off Keep audio to remove audio and keep only numbers and transcripts. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Spoke collects no data from anyone, including children.
